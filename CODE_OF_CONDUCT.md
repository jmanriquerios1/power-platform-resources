# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and practical technical community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Share knowledge openly.
- Focus on real-world engineering value.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination in any form.
- Disruptive or hostile communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or moderate content that violates this code.
